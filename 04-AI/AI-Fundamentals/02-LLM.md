## LLM (Large Language Model)

It is basically like a **brain trained with a large amount of data** to understand, generate, summarize, translate, and answer questions in human language.

### Interview Definition

> A Large Language Model (LLM) is a type of Deep Learning model, based on Transformer neural networks, that is trained on huge amounts of text data to understand and generate human-like language.

### Examples of LLMs
- ChatGPT (GPT-4, GPT-5)
- Gemini
- Claude
- Llama
- Mistral
- DeepSeek

### Key capability: In-context learning
The model can learn how to do a task from the examples and instructions you give **inside the prompt**, without being retrained.
